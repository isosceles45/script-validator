# Campaign Brief — Tea Tree Pore Ampoule

**Campaign:** #PoreCheck — Q4 creator activation
**Product:** TFS Tea Tree Pore Ampoule
**Channel:** TikTok / Instagram Reels, 30–45 seconds, vertical

## Objective
Drive trial of the Tea Tree Pore Ampoule among first-time TFS buyers by
positioning it as the honest, no-hype entry point into pore care.

## Target audience
18–24, oily and combination skin, acne-prone. Skincare-literate: they read
ingredient lists and are actively sceptical of overclaiming. They have been
burned by "miracle" products before.

## Key message
Real pore care is gradual and ingredient-led, not an overnight fix. The ampoule
uses a stabilised BHA that is gentler than conventional salicylic acid.

## Tone of voice
Honest, conversational, peer-to-peer. Never clinical, never a hard sell.
Understatement beats hype with this audience — if it sounds like an ad, it fails.

## Mandatories
- Say the full product name at least once
- Show the texture on camera
- Include the hashtag #PoreCheck
- End with a clear CTA to the link in bio
- Do not make medical or acne-treatment claims
